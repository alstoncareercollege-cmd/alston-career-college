About the Institution

Alston Career College is a private postsecondary career college committed to providing high-quality, practical career education to adult learners. Through structured classroom instruction, supervised hands-on training, and education grounded in real-world industry experience, the college strives to develop knowledgeable, skilled, analytically and clinically discerning, ethical, and career-ready professionals in the fields it teaches.
