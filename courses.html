Courses

Alston Career College currently offers one course, described below. As a career college, Alston Career College may add additional courses in the future; any new course will be added here, and to the institution's catalog, only after it has received the required state approvals.

Massage Therapy Program

588 Hours of Instruction Certificate of Completion

Alston Career College is committed to providing high-quality massage therapy education to adult learners who are seeking to enter or advance in the massage and wellness profession. Through structured classroom instruction, supervised hands-on training, and education grounded in real-world industry experience, the college strives to develop knowledgeable, skilled, analytically and clinically discerning, ethical, and career-ready massage professionals.

Total scheduled program time is 600 hours: 588 hours of instruction, 4 hours of new-student orientation, and 8 hours of scheduled examinations.
